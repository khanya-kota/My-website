## Project Overview

Green Roots Organics is a South African organic fruit and vegetable delivery service. 
The business was founded in 2018 in Port Elizabeth by two local farmers.

The website is designed to provide customers with information about the company,
its products and services, organic farming, subscriptions, and online ordering.

The website aims to create a natural, fresh, trustworthy and user-friendly online
experience for customers.

---

## Website Goals

The main goals of the website are:

- Increase online orders and subscription sign-ups.
- Generate leads through newsletter sign-ups and enquiry forms.
- Provide information about organic farming and healthy living.
- Build customer trust through product information and company content.
- Provide an easy and convenient online shopping experience.

The website aims to increase online orders and subscription sign-ups by 40% within
six months.

---

## Target Audience

The website is aimed at:

- Health-conscious families aged 25–50.
- Working professionals.
- Small cafés and restaurants.
- Customers who value organic and locally grown produce.
- Customers who prefer convenient home delivery.

## Website Pages

### 1. Home Page - `index.html`

The homepage introduces Green Roots Organics and provides:

- Hero banner
- "Shop Now" call-to-action
- Featured products
- Customer testimonials
- Navigation to other sections of the website

### 2. About Page - `about.html`

The About page provides information about:

- The history of Green Roots Organics
- The company's mission
- The company's vision
- Farm information
- Company photographs

### 3. Context Page - `context.html`

The Context page provides relevant information about the business,
its products, services and organic farming approach.

### 4. Descriptive Page - `descriptive.html`

The Descriptive page provides descriptions and information about the
products and services offered by Green Roots Organics.

### 5. Enquiry Page - `enquiry.html`

The Enquiry page allows users to contact the business and submit enquiries.
